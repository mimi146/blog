Hi, I’m **Milan Niroula**, a software engineer.

This blog is my notebook in public. I read a lot about **AI**, **software engineering**, **design**, and **how big tech companies solve problems at scale**, and I write here to understand those ideas better. Most posts are research notes or write-ups of articles and papers I’ve read. I summarize the main ideas, pull out what I think matters, and add my own take.

## What you’ll find here

- **AI.** Notes on models, tools, and how AI is changing the way software gets built.
- **Software engineering.** Architecture, reliability, testing, and the craft of writing good code.
- **Design.** Interfaces, product thinking, and why some systems feel good to use.
- **Systems at scale.** How large engineering organizations handle traffic, data, and complexity.
- **The inner life.** I’m also a spiritual person, and now and then I write about attention, discipline, and meaning, and how they connect to the work.
